# Mechanism Patterns

Use this reference when selecting mechanism families or generating alternatives.

## Common Families

### Hinged Door or Flap

Best for visible open/close access, protective covers, sample ports, battery doors, and small closures.

Watch for hinge pin support, stop geometry, finger clearance, overtravel, creep in living hinges, latch engagement, and debris around the hinge line.

### Sliding Door or Shuttle

Best when vertical space is tight or motion should stay within the product envelope.

Watch for rail friction, jamming, debris, tolerance stack across rails, need for detents, and user perception of looseness.

### Snap Fit and Latch Hook

Best for low-cost assembly, covers, bezels, cartridges, and one-time or low-cycle closure.

Watch for strain limit, lead-in angle, release access, creep, brittle materials, stress whitening, and tolerance sensitivity.

### Cam, Ramp, and Wedge

Best for converting insertion motion into lifting, clamping, flap opening, switch actuation, or seal compression.

Watch for friction, wear, self-locking angle, force spike, lubrication, debris, and need for hard stops.

### Spring Return

Best for doors, ejectors, contacts, latches, buttons, and repeatable return motion.

Watch for preload, fatigue, buckling, noise, corrosion, assembly handling, and user force budget.

### Flexure

Best for small compliant motion, snap domes, low-part-count clips, and low-travel mechanisms.

Watch for material fatigue, creep, strain concentration, temperature sensitivity, and limited travel.

### Magnetic Coupling or Closure

Best for premium feel, gentle alignment, removable covers, and sealed outer skins.

Watch for cost, magnet retention, interference with sensors, polarity mistakes, demagnetization, and regulatory/electrical constraints.

### Screw, Boss, and Insert Structure

Best for serviceable assemblies, structural joints, and controlled clamp loads.

Watch for boss cracking, thread engagement, insert heat staking process, sink marks, screw access, and torque limits.

### Seal, Gasket, and Compression Rib

Best for dust/liquid protection, sample containment, odor control, and user confidence.

Watch for compression set, chemical compatibility, flash, parting line leakage, friction during assembly, and tolerance-controlled compression.

## Selection Heuristic

Use the simplest family that satisfies function, user action, life cycle, and manufacturing method:

1. Can a fixed guide and chamfer solve it without a mechanism?
2. Can a molded flexure, snap, or latch solve it without extra parts?
3. Is a spring needed for return, force, or user feedback?
4. Is a cam/ramp needed to convert one user motion into another motion?
5. Is a screw/insert needed for service, strength, or repeatability?
6. Is the added mechanism still robust after tolerance, contamination, and assembly checks?

## Concept Route Template

For each route, specify:

- Principle: hinge, slide, cam, flexure, latch, spring, magnet, seal, screw, or hybrid.
- User action: push, pull, rotate, insert, press, twist, remove, or replace.
- Moving parts and fixed parts.
- Stops and constraints.
- Materials and manufacturing route.
- Biggest risk and the first test.
